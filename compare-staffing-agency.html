Jobility vs. a staffing agency, in short.

Jobility is a direct hiring platform: it finds and vets candidates, and you hire the person onto your own payroll. Its placement fee is 8% of first-year salary when you post and schedule, or 15% when Jobility’s team does the work, charged only when you hire. The most common agency direct-hire fee in 2021 was 20%,1 and for it a recruiter usually runs the search. Jobility also sells job posts from $150, with no fee when you hire.

When a staffing agency is the better choice.

Choose an agency, or a full-service firm like our sister company Jobility Talent Solutions, if:

  • You want someone else to run the whole search, including the interviews.
  • You have no time to review candidates or interview in the weeks ahead.
  • The role is senior or rare enough that you want a recruiter approaching people who aren’t looking for work.

Held until Q14: “You need a replacement guarantee and Jobility’s terms don’t give you one.”

Choose Jobility if:

  • You can interview and make the offer yourself (self-service, 8%), or you want our team to do the work for less than a typical agency fee (Jobility managed, 15%).
  • You want to see the price before you start: a placement fee of 8% or 15% that you pay only when you hire, or a flat price per post from $150.
  • You also need temp or shift workers, from the same account.

For direct hires, Jobility is a hiring platform, not a staffing agency: the person you choose joins your own payroll, and you pay a one-time placement fee or a post price instead of an ongoing markup. For temp and shift work, Jobility can be the worker’s employer of record and run their payroll, which is how a staffing agency works too. If you want a full-service agency to run the whole search, our sister company, Jobility Talent Solutions, does that.

Frequently asked questions

See all questions

Jobility is a direct hiring platform. You post a job once, and Jobility finds, vets and shortlists candidates, so you get workers, not resumes. You can hire full-time and part-time staff directly, bring on temp and contract workers with Jobility as the employer of record, or cover shifts, all from one account.

Jobility charges either a flat price per job post, from $150, or a placement fee of 8% or 15% that you pay only when you hire. Job posts cost $150 for general labor and light industrial roles, $250 for IT, and $200 to $500 for healthcare roles; you pay once when you publish, and there’s no fee when you hire.

The placement fee is 8% if you post and schedule, or 15% if we do the work and send you a candidate, and posting on that plan is free. The fee is a percentage of first-year salary; for hourly roles, we convert the pay rate to a yearly amount. Teams that post every month can ask about a subscription. Temp and shift workers are quoted by the hour. For post prices and placement fees, see Jobility pricing.

A job board shows your post to job seekers and sends you everyone who applies; Jobility screens candidates against your requirements and sends you a short list. There is no charge per click or per applicant: you pay per post from $150, or post free and pay a placement fee only when you hire. Per hire, you pay less than you would on a job board or with a staffing agency, because you aren’t paying for clicks and applicants and then sorting them yourself.

For direct hires, Jobility is a hiring platform, not a staffing agency: the person you choose joins your own payroll, and you pay a one-time placement fee or a post price instead of an ongoing markup. For temp and shift work, Jobility can be the worker’s employer of record and run their payroll, which is how a staffing agency works too. If you want a full-service agency to run the whole search, our sister company, Jobility Talent Solutions, does that.

It depends on how you hire. Direct hires work for you: they join your payroll from day one. For temp, contract and shift workers, Jobility can be the employer of record. Choose it when you post. Jobility then runs their payroll and taxes and covers workers’ comp while you direct the work. You can hire a temp or shift worker onto your staff. No conversion fee after 480 hours (about three months). Before 480 hours, an 8% fee of the salary applies.

On the placement-fee plan, you pay nothing unless you hire. On pay per post, talk to us and a person on our team will look at your post with you.

In order to be matched Gigees are required to submit to a Basic Background Check and upload the following information into the Platform: the industry and types of Gigs they would like to work, their availability, the minimum hourly rate they desire, and the distance they are willing to travel for a Gig. JOBILITY will then pair vetted, qualified Gigees to Gigs that suit their location, availability, and experience. If the Gigee’s availability matches a Hirer’s Gig details, they will be matched in JOBILITY’s system and the Gigee will be given the option to apply for the Gig. The Hirer will then have the option to accept the Gigee of choice or to not accept anyone and the Gig will expire or can be cancelled by the Hirer.

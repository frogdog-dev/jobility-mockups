What happens after you post.

Four steps, then one line on when you pay, plan by plan.

Add every location to your profile and post to any of them, and save templates for roles you hire often.

  1. Step 1

    You post. Your job goes live.

  2. Step 2

    Matches arrive on your dashboard.

    Each one is screened against your requirements.

  3. Step 3

    You choose: interview, shortlist or pass.

  4. Step 4

    You hire.

    Direct hires join your payroll. Temp and shift workers can work for Jobility instead, which runs their payroll and taxes (this is called being the employer of record). You choose it when you post.

  5. When you pay

    Then you pay.

    Per post: once, when you post it. Placement fee: only after you hire, on the date set in your terms. Temp and shift workers: by the hour, at the rate we quote before anyone starts.

It depends on how you hire. Direct hires work for you: they join your payroll from day one. For temp, contract and shift workers, Jobility can be the employer of record, which runs their payroll and taxes while you direct the work. You choose it when you post. Jobility also carries workers’ comp.

An employer of record is the company that legally employs a worker: it runs payroll and withholds taxes, while you direct the day-to-day work. For a small business, that means you can bring on temp, contract or shift workers without setting up payroll for them.

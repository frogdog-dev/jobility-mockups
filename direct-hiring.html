Questions about direct hiring

The difference is the split of work. An agency recruiter typically runs the search and the first interviews, and often offers a replacement guarantee. On the 15% placement fee, we do the work and send you a candidate. On the 8% fee, you post and schedule, and you run the interviews yourself. With either, you pay only when you hire.

Jobility vs. a staffing agency, in full

Choose pay per post if you want one fixed price, paid up front, with no fee when you hire. Choose the placement fee if you’d rather pay nothing up front and pay only if you hire. The quote on this page shows both for your role and salary.

8%: you post and schedule. 15%: we do the work and send you a candidate. Both are a share of first-year salary, AI-driven matching finds and screens candidates either way, and you pay only when you hire. Hourly roles: we convert the pay rate to a yearly amount.

You pay Jobility’s placement fee only after you hire someone we introduced, on the terms you agree when you sign up. Posting on the placement-fee plan is free. Held until Q14: the payment date, how long an introduction counts, and how you pay.

On the placement-fee plan, you pay nothing unless you hire. On pay per post, talk to us and a person on our team will look at your post with you.

Questions about temp and contract workers

See all questions

An employer of record is the company that legally employs a worker: it runs payroll and withholds taxes, while you direct the day-to-day work. For a small business, that means you can bring on temp or contract workers without setting up payroll for them or handling their employment paperwork. You choose Jobility as the employer of record when you post, and Jobility also covers workers’ comp. What stays with you: directing the work, approving hours and keeping the workplace safe.

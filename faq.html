Jobility vs. a staffing agency, in full

A job board shows your post to job seekers and sends you everyone who applies; Jobility screens candidates against your requirements and sends you a short list. There is no charge per click or per applicant: you pay per post from $150, or post free and pay a placement fee only when you hire. Per hire, you pay less than you would on a job board or with a staffing agency, because you aren’t paying for clicks and applicants and then sorting them yourself.

Jobility charges either a flat price per job post, from $150, or a placement fee of 8% or 15% that you pay only when you hire. Job posts cost $150 for general labor and light industrial roles, $250 for IT, and $200 to $500 for healthcare roles; you pay once, when you publish. Buy one job posting, get one free. Exclusive offer for new customers. Temp and shift workers are quoted by the hour.

Who employs the worker

6 questions

It depends on how you hire. Direct hires work for you: they join your payroll from day one. For temp, contract and shift workers, Jobility can be the employer of record, which runs their payroll and taxes while you direct the work. You choose it when you post. As employer of record, Jobility also covers workers’ comp.

An employer of record is the company that legally employs a worker: it runs payroll and withholds taxes, while you direct the day-to-day work. For a small business, that means you can bring on temp, contract or shift workers without setting up payroll for them. Jobility can be the employer of record for temp, contract and shift workers, and you choose it when you post. What stays with you: directing the work and keeping the workplace safe.

It means Jobility is the shift worker’s employer: it runs payroll and taxes and covers workers’ comp for the shifts they work. You choose it when you post, and you still set the shift and direct the work.

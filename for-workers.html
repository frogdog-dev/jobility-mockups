How Jobility works for you.

  1. 1

    Build your profile.

    In the app or on the web. Add your skills, experience, availability and how far you’ll travel. In the app, you’re a Gigee.

  2. 2

    Get checked.

    We check your skills, availability and work history. Healthcare workers add their license and certifications.

  3. 3

    Get matched.

    We send you shifts and jobs that fit. Accept the ones you want.

  4. 4

    Work and get paid.

    Clock in and out in the app. Pay arrives on a set schedule.

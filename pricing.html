Jobility vs. a staffing agency.

Fee
In a Staffing Industry Analysts survey (2021 data), 20% of first-year salary was the most common agency direct-hire fee.1 Jobility’s placement fee is 15% when our team does the work and sends you a candidate, or 8% when you post and schedule yourself. Most agencies, like Jobility’s placement fee, charge only when you hire.
Who does the work
An agency recruiter runs the search and the interviews. On Jobility managed, our team does the work and sends you a candidate. On self-service, you post and schedule, and we find and screen candidates.

Jobility vs. job boards.

A job board gets you applicants. Jobility gets you a short list of vetted people.
